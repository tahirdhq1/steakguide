🥩 What Is Rump Steak?

Rump steak comes from the hindquarter of the cow. It is lean, flavourful, and more affordable than premium cuts like ribeye.

Because it contains less fat, it can become tough if overcooked. That’s why monitoring rump steak internal temperature is essential.

🌡 Why Internal Temperature Matters

Cooking steak is about heat control.

If temperature is too low, the Steak remains undercooked.

If temperature is too high:

  • Proteins tighten
  • Moisture escapes
  • The steak becomes chewy

Using a thermometer removes guesswork and improves consistency.

🌡 Rump Steak Internal Temperature Chart (°C)

Below is the recommended temperature guide for rump steak in the UK.

Doneness Internal Temp (°C) Appearance Texture
Rare 50°C Bright red centre Very soft
Medium Rare 55°C Warm red centre Tender
Medium 60°C Pink centre Firm
Medium Well 65°C Slight pink Slightly firm
Well Done 70°C+ Brown throughout Firm

Chef Recommendation: For rump steak, medium rare (55°C) gives the best balance of tenderness and flavour.

🥩 Doneness Levels Explained

Rare (50°C)

  • Soft and juicy
  • Deep red inside
  • Strong beef flavour

Medium Rare (55°C)

  • Warm red centre
  • Juicy and tender
  • Most recommended level

Medium (60°C)

  • Pink centre
  • Firmer texture
  • Less juice

Medium Well (65°C)

  • Slight pink
  • Noticeably firm
  • Less moisture

Well Done (70°C+)

  • Brown throughout
  • Firm texture
  • Minimal juice

⏱ Cooking Time by Thickness

Cooking time depends on steak thickness, not just temperature.

For a 2.5cm thick rump steak:

  • Rare: 2–3 minutes per side
  • Medium rare: 3 minutes per side
  • Medium: 4 minutes per side

Always confirm with a thermometer.

🌡 How to Check Steak Temperature Correctly

  1. Insert thermometer into the thickest part.
  2. Avoid touching bone or fat.
  3. Check near the centre.
  4. Remove steak 2–3°C before target temperature.

Digital instant-read thermometers are most accurate.

🔥 Carryover Cooking Explained

When you remove steak from heat, it continues cooking. This is called carryover cooking.

Temperature usually rises 2–5°C after removal.

If you want 55°C medium rare, remove the steak at 52–53°C. This prevents overcooking.

🏥 UK Food Safety Guidance

According to the Food Standards Agency, whole cuts of beef can be served rare if properly handled and seared on the outside.

  • Ensure beef is fresh
  • Cook the exterior thoroughly
  • Avoid serving rare to vulnerable groups

For high-risk individuals, cooking to 70°C ensures safety.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Cutting steak immediately
  • Guessing doneness by colour
  • Using low heat
  • Ignoring carryover cooking
  • Using thin steaks

❓ FAQs

What temperature is rump steak medium rare?

Medium rare rump steak is 55°C internal temperature.

Is 60°C too high for rump steak?

60°C gives medium doneness. It is slightly firmer but still acceptable.

Is it safe to eat rump steak rare in the UK?

Yes, if sourced fresh and seared properly.

How much does steak temperature rise while resting?

It usually rises 2–5°C due to carryover cooking.

Do I need a thermometer?

Yes. It ensures consistent and accurate results.
